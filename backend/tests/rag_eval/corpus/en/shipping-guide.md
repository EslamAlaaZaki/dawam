# Shipping guide

## Delivery times
Standard orders are delivered within 5 business days. Express orders are delivered the next business day when placed before noon.

## Returning an item
To return an item, print the label from your order page and drop the parcel at any collection point. The refund request must include the order number.

## Backup couriers
If the main courier is unavailable, orders are kept for 35 days at the depot before being sent back.
