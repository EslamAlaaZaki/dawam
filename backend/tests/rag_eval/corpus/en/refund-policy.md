# Refund policy

## Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. The request must include the order number and the reason for the refund. Digital downloads that were already opened are not eligible.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 10 business days. Bank transfers can take up to 5 extra days to appear on the statement.

## Shipping costs
Shipping fees are refunded only when the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent. Return shipping for a change of mind is paid by the customer.

## Exceptions
Custom-made products and gift cards cannot be refunded. Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time but the current billing period is not refunded.
