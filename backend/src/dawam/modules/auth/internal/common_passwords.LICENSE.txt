common_passwords.txt: where it comes from and its licence
=========================================================

common_passwords.txt is the password list of zxcvbn (https://github.com/dropbox/zxcvbn,
data/passwords.txt at commit 3bf5a6cf3aa916dabb4c4e73a6ae24277ab36170): the most common
passwords of Mark Burnett's public 10 million password corpus, most common first. DAWAM
keeps only the passwords themselves (the counts are dropped), lower-cased, without
duplicates. zxcvbn is distributed under the MIT licence below, which is compatible with
DAWAM's Apache License 2.0; this notice must stay with the file.
